LensPipe

Low-latency local studio camera for PC and OBS — iPhone & Android, no app, no cloud, no account.

Built for me, shared with all.

Streams your phone's camera (iPhone or Android) straight to your PC and OBS over USB or Wi-Fi/LAN using WebCodecs and binary WebSockets. Everything stays on your local network.

Step by step

Configure in OBS Studio

  1. Add Browser Source: In OBS, open Sources → + → Browser and name it LensPipe.
  2. Paste URL & canvas size: Paste the URL above and set Width × Height to 3840 × 2160 (or your OBS canvas, e.g. 1920 × 1080). Leave Custom CSS empty.
  3. Phone audio (optional): Check Control audio via OBS for a dedicated mixer fader, then turn on Phone mic in LensPipe.
  4. Scene switching & live control: Optionally enable Refresh browser when scene becomes active. Lens, zoom, aspect, and EV changes apply live.
⚠ Blank or black source in OBS?

Run once in PowerShell from the LensPipe folder, then restart OBS:

Import-Certificate -FilePath '.\certs\local-cert.pem' -CertStoreLocation Cert:\CurrentUser\Root Only trust a certificate generated on your own PC.
